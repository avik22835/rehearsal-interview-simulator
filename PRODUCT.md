# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One software engineering student practising on a Windows laptop with 16 GB RAM
and no dedicated GPU. The task is focused interview preparation across coding,
projects, system design, backend, AI engineering and behavioral questions.

## Product Purpose

An engaging, complete interview practice workspace with adaptive conversation,
practical tools, honest evidence-based feedback, and saved progress. Strategic
context comes from the user-confirmed PROJECT-BRIEF.md and docs/product-spec.md.

## Brand Personality

Focused, encouraging, precise. Smooth and approachable enough for daily study.

## Anti-references

The confirmed brief rejects boring study experiences, disconnected demos,
overwhelming active-question screens, and visual polish deferred to the end.
Avoid decorative dashboards or invented progress metrics that conceal missing work.

## Design Principles

- Keep the active question and the candidate's work prominent.
- Make interview state and saved work legible.
- Let evidence justify feedback; acknowledge missing information.
- Keep optional context inspectable without overwhelming the conversation.
- Build one consistent workspace across all six modes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, readable text, labelled controls, reduced motion,
responsive laptop layouts, and a complete typed alternative to voice.
